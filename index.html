Quick Reference: WHtR Body Composition Scoring

Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) Rules

WHtR = Waist (inches) ÷ Height (inches). Same thresholds for all ages and genders:

  • ≤ 0.49 (Low Risk): 20.0 pts
  • 0.50 (Moderate): 19.0 pts
  • 0.51: 18.0  |  0.52: 17.0  |  0.53: 16.0  |  0.54: 15.0
  • 0.55: 12.5  |  0.56: 10.0  |  0.57: 7.5  |  0.58: 5.0  |  0.59: 2.5
  • ≥ 0.60 (High Risk): 0 pts — automatic body composition program enrollment risk
