Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) Rules

The updated system scores body composition on target ratios (Waist divided by Height):

  • Low Risk Ratio (≤ 0.49): 20.0 Max points earned.
  • Moderate Risk Ratio (0.50 to 0.55): 17.0 points earned.
  • High Risk Ratio (> 0.55): 0 points earned. Ratios above 0.55 risk an immediate body composition tracking enrollment.
