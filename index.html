Quick Reference Scoring

2-Mile Run

The 2-Mile Run is the primary measure of cardiorespiratory fitness. Maximum points: 50. Minimum passing points: 29.5. Failing to meet the minimum time for your age/gender bracket results in an immediate component failure, regardless of your total composite score.

1-Minute Push-ups

Push-ups measure your upper-body muscular fitness. Maximum points: 15. Minimum passing points: 7.5. Proper form requires breaking 90 degrees at the elbow and locking out at the top. Sagging or bowing your back will result in terminated reps.

1-Minute Sit-ups

Sit-ups are a measure of core endurance. Maximum points: 15. Minimum passing points: 7.5. Your elbows must touch your knees/thighs at the top of the movement, and your shoulder blades must touch the mat at the bottom. Your hands must remain in contact with your chest/shoulders.
